# 🚀 Projevo AI

### AI-Powered Personalized Engineering Project Mentor

Projevo AI is an AI-powered platform designed to support engineering students throughout their complete project lifecycle — from understanding their skills and discovering suitable projects to improving projects, getting support during development, and preparing for project-based viva and technical interviews.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Engineering students often face difficulties throughout the project development process.

They may struggle with:

- Choosing a project that matches their skills and interests
- Evaluating whether their own project idea is feasible
- Identifying the skills they are missing
- Improving an existing project
- Detecting code quality, security, accessibility, and performance issues
- Getting timely help when they are stuck during development
- Preparing for project-based viva and technical interviews

Most solutions address only one part of this journey. Projevo AI aims to provide personalized support throughout the entire project lifecycle.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Projevo AI creates a personalized experience based on the student's:

- Skills and skill levels
- Interests
- Academic background
- Domain
- Career goals
- Project details

It then provides AI-powered guidance based on the student's current knowledge and project requirements.
